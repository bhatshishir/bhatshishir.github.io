This is what I learnt about Cash
Tags: Investment

Always have 5-15% cash with you for it to be deployed at the right time. You shall be tempted to pour in all the cash and go all in in the bull run or maybe the assumed bull run. This cash can vary from 5% in the bull run to 15-20% after booking profits in the bull run and keep it handy. I take inspiration from Nassim Taleb's Barbell Strategy. Go 80% safe and long term and 20% for those high risk - high return types. I have tweaked it for my risk appetite and go with the below strategy.
1. 70% - Long term bets with a compounding platform with a low downside.
2. 20% - Swing Trades to capture some alpha.( The hit rate is 6/10)
3. 10%  - A boom or bust trade like a Biotech or a Pharma Innovator types.

The first 2 years, I have barely made 5-10% returns. I know it is very low and all of you reading this would say, "You should've put your money in the Index Funds" or "FD is better" or even better "What are you high on?!". But you know what if you are not invested in the market, then how on earth would you even learn how it works. Not a single fund manager who's outperformed the markets on a streak have done it by their first day. Its a process. You have to figure out whether your process leads to the right outcome or not. Sometimes even the wrong process leads to a good outcome out of sheer luck. So you've gotta be careful and evaluate stuff. Investing is more about understanding and discovering your weaknesses and flaws as a person.

Cash is very important. When you feel the valuations are absurdly high, then sell it in tranches and raise cash. This valuation absurdity is not about the market as a whole but this occurs in sectors as a whole and individual companies. Always ask "What could go wrong?". Read more about Nassim Taleb. Antifragile and Black Swan teaches you something more about creating a situation/mindset/portfolio where you/your portfolio shall arise from distress and downturn. for all this the safety net is your cash. When the highest conviction bet of yours falls down 10-20%, that is when you deploy that cash.

You have to develop a system. But don't be rigid, because the market is like an amoeba, it transmutates its characteristics. You should be able to update your system as evidence keeps changing. But the fundamental things like the capital cycle and market cycle will never change.

For some evidence, the broader markets in India + the Nifty are correcting. I have my cash and I am slowly deploying it in tranches. Something different is happening this time. The Nifty is correcting and the SMID isn't. when the Nifty tries to recover the SMID corrects. The market is becoming bipolar. Let's watch what happens.
