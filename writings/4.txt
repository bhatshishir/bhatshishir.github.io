I will figure it out.
Tags: Life

You sensed that you should be following a different path, a more ambitious one, you felt that you were destined for other things but you had no idea how to achieve them and in your misery you began to hate everything around you. - Dostoevsky

Yes, I will figure it out as I always had. Yesterday on Vijaya Dashami, I officially resigned my Software Development job. I was always passionate to get into a finance role. And fortunately when I moved to Mumbai, I met a friend who got into Equity Research with a Mechanical Engineering background and had previously worked as a Software Developer for a year. After meeting him I got the hope that I can get into finance without an MBA. I tried researching about companies, reading concalls and annual reports, and then attended a cohort for Equity Research at The Valuation School. A big shoutout to Parth Verma. An incredible teacher!

I have a notice period of around 45 days until december 1st week and I don't have any job at hand to switch. I have been looking for an equity research job for 3 months now and got no responses. I have already connected to a lot of people on Linkedin and cold messaged them showcasing my 36 page report on Aarti Pharmalabs only. I was basically asking for a feedback. But didn't get people's attention. So after a while, I changed the strategy and made a 3 pager on the same company, came up with my Equity Research resume and sending the same people. Now the hit rate increased. But I wasn't satisfied. Changed the strategy and wrote 2 pager thesis on 2 more companies that I already researched, Shilpa Medicare and Bajaj Healthcare. Now, sent it to the same people and some new ones as well, the hit rate increased. Most of them told there are no vacancies. It is fair because even the market is going through a tough phase due to macro economic factors. 

What I have realised is that, I can't increase the hit rate from here on. I have messaged around 400 people and 42 have replied , that's around 10%. Now, I just have to increase the sample size, to 1000 and with the same hit rate that would be 100 replies and hope to see 10 people telling there is a vacancy and I hope I get 5-6 offers at hand and select the best one. 

This is the strategy and it should work in the next 2 months atleast or else I would be without a job. But this is my calling and its worth all the thing in life, so I'm taking the shot. I have made sure the downsides are capped, I have an emergency fund to cover 4-5 months of my expenses after I leave my current company post the notice period. 

I don't want to be so humble that I ignore my own voices in my head!

Will keep you updated :)
